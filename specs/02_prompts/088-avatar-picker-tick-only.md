[88] The selection becomes the tick, and only the tick.
[87]'s tick was right; the thicker border and hard shadow shipped beside it were not. A hard shadow
means *pressable* in this design system, and every tile in that row is pressable. Worse, the shadow
colour inverts to nearly white in dark mode, so the marker failed in exactly the scheme the original
bug lived in. The tile is now identical in both states and a drawn tick is the whole signal.

Its full specification is in `ui-exp02-design-review-rounds.md`, round F — that file
consolidates the consecutive design-review rounds, because they cut across many tasks at once
and reading them one at a time loses the argument that connects them.
